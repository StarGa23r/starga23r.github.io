HMMWV Pi rclone

HMMWV Pi rclone is a personal, single-user app. It lets Ben’s own Raspberry Pi upload security-camera photos to his own Google Drive using rclone.

The app is used only by its owner. It does not offer accounts to other people, and it does not share Google user data with anyone else.
